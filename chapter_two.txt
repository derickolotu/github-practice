If you haven't figured it out yet, I was born in space.

 

More exactly, I was born on an intergalactic station. Not that I spent much time at the station. The majority of my childhood I spent as a co-pilot. Taking second seat to my father as he would patrol the cosmos in his ship, the Toyota. Together we would jump between galaxies, going from one adventure to the next. Each mission became a potential expedition into the unknown.

 

Such was the excitement of being a Galactic Ranger. Each time we left the station, we never knew what kind of day we would have.
